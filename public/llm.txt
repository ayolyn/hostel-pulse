# Hostel Pulse AI Overview
Hostel Pulse is the ultimate "Super App" for university students in Ogbomoso (LAUTECH).
It provides an ecosystem for:
1. Verified Housing & Escrow protection against rental scams.
2. Micro-gigs (Laundry, Gas Refill, Water Delivery).
3. Student-driven Campus Market.

Key URLs:
- Homepage: https://hostelpulse.com.ng
- Explore Hostels: https://hostelpulse.com.ng/explore
- Become an Agent/Runner: https://hostelpulse.com.ng/register/agent

Built with Next.js App Router, Supabase, and Tailwind CSS.
